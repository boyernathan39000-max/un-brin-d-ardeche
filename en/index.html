The house

A stone house in the middle of meadows and woods

South-facing with no neighbours, the house is surrounded by meadows, woods and forest. A river runs at the bottom of the grounds and the little birds will be your neighbours.

You have the whole house to yourselves: four bedrooms, a living room with a fully equipped kitchen, a large partly covered terrace and a garden.

Activities

Things to do around Saint-Prix

Lamastre is 8 km away and Le Cheylard 10 km. Here are four ideas for outings nearby.

Less than 1 km

La Dolce Via

Cycle or stroll along a safe, purpose-built greenway. The stretch from Le Cheylard to Lamastre is almost 20 km long.

Departing from Lamastre

The Mastrou steam train

Travel by steam train over 28 km, from Lamastre to Tournon-sur-Rhône, through the Doux gorges. The journey crosses bridges, viaducts and tunnels.

40 minutes by car

The Doux gorges

A wild setting for rail biking. The area also offers swimming and white-water sports.

Pays de Lamastre

Hiking and nature

Choose from 22 walking trails and 3 free orienteering courses to do as a family.
